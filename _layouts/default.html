Ajinkya Jain

Hi! I am a Senior Research Scientist at Google (Intrinsic), where I am part of the R&D team led by Prof. Stefan Schaal. My research lies at the intersection of robot learning, embodied foundation models, and physics-informed control, focusing on methods that enable robots to perform dexterous, contact-rich, and multi-robot manipulation reliably in the real world. Recently, my work has centered on on-policy distillation and reinforcement learning finetuning, policy synthesis with foundation models (VLMs/LLMs), and force-aware imitation and online learning for tight-tolerance assembly.

Previously, I was a Robotics Engineer on the R&D team at Vicarious (acquired by Alphabet in 2022 and integrated into Intrinsic). I received my Ph.D. in Robotics from The University of Texas at Austin under the guidance of Prof. Scott Niekum and Prof. Ashish Deshpande, where my doctoral research focused on learning object articulation and hybrid interaction models from visual data and motion planning under uncertainty. Prior to UT Austin, I earned my Dual Degree (B.Tech.–M.Tech.) in Mechanical Engineering (Robotics and Automation) from IIT Kanpur.

Research

My research develops scalable learning and control algorithms for generalist, contact-rich robotic manipulation—spanning reinforcement learning finetuning and policy distillation, foundation models (VLMs/LLMs) for policy synthesis, force-aware imitation and online learning, sim-to-real transfer, and motion planning under uncertainty. Alongside my publications and patented industrial manipulation technology (US Patent 12,330,304 B2), I co-organize community workshops at RSS 2026, IROS 2026, and IEEE Humanoids 2026 on sim-to-real transfer and learning-based whole-body robot control.
